# Demo workspace

The demo workspace is a ready-made library of invented notes that you can explore and change freely. It works completely offline and needs no AI model.

## Open the demo

In the workspace window choose **Try demo**. Manifold creates the demo inside its own application-data folder, separate from your own workspaces, and opens it. It appears in the recent list like any other workspace. Open **Welcome** first, then **Tour of Manifold**.

## What is in it

Everything in the demo is sample data. Nothing comes from a real person's notes, and nothing is downloaded.

- About eighty notes: mathematics (with formulas), science, programming exercises in several languages, study skills, fictional books with properties, projects with dated tasks, two weeks of daily notes, meeting notes and reference pages.
- Cards and quizzes made from some of the notes.
- A PDF to read and highlight.
- A canvas that maps the ideas of the study notes.
- Two Bases views, one for reading and one for projects.
- Property types, a few sample calendar events and tasks, and sample items in the Assistant's memory.

## Seven things to try

The **Tour of Manifold** note walks through seven short flows. Each works offline:

1. **Open and search**: find a note with the quick switcher (⌘O) and with a search such as a tag. See [Search](SEARCH.md).
2. **Links and backlinks**: read the Backlinks section of a note and open the graph. See [Links & backlinks](LINKS-AND-BACKLINKS.md).
3. **Properties and Bases**: look at the property types and at a Bases view built from the book notes. See [Properties & tags](PROPERTIES-AND-TAGS.md).
4. **Cards and quiz**: review the cards of a note and take its quiz.
5. **Maths**: check a step-by-step derivation, including a note that contains a wrong step on purpose.
6. **Code**: run the tests of a programming exercise in the sandbox. See [Code workbench](CODE-WORKBENCH.md).
7. **PDF**: select a sentence in the PDF and highlight it. The highlight is saved as text in a note next to the PDF.

## AI in the demo

No model is installed in the demo and none is requested. AI features show as **Trial** until a model has been measured on your Mac, and trial results are never written into your notes without asking. See [AI & models](AI-AND-MODELS.md).

## Your edits and the reset

The demo is safe to edit.

- Opening it again never overwrites your edits and never brings back a note you deleted.
- **Reset** puts the demo back to its original state. It asks for confirmation first. It saves a snapshot before it starts, so the previous state is one undo away, and it restores every original note. Notes you added yourself are not deleted: they are moved into a dated folder inside the workspace's `.manifold` folder. Sample calendar events, Assistant memory and learning progress are set back too.
- Reset works only in the demo, never in your own workspaces.

> [!note] Still being finished
> The demo is growing. The user guide pages (the pages you are reading) are being added to it as notes in a **Manifold Guide** folder, and the place in the app where you start a reset is being finalised.

## Remove the demo

Remove **Manifold Demo** from the recent list with **Remove from list**. This forgets the entry and does not delete any files.
